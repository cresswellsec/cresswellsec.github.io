2210 IT Jobs

An at-a-glance table of every open federal IT Specialist (series 2210) job on USAJOBS: dates, agency, title, grade, location and a link to each posting.

Keyword search plus “vector similarity” results computed at build time from pre-made embeddings — no server, no database, no AI model in the browser. A separate data pipeline fetches and cleans USAJOBS data, chunks and embeds it, and publishes a dataset; the site is rebuilt from fresh data with one command.
